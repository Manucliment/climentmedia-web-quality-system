What I do in your Google Ads account

  • Search and Shopping

    The campaigns that reach people already looking for what you sell. Keywords, search terms and budgets, with the account read every day.

  • Performance Max

    Google pushes it because that is where Google earns more, and it doesn't always say clearly what it does. I use it when I know why and when, after testing, and I tell you what I'm turning on and what I'm turning off.

  • Conversions that mean something

    Before optimising for a conversion, I check what it counts. In the first weeks I ask you which leads were good, not just how many came in.

  • The software I work with

    Climent Ads Assistant, which I built. It reads Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4 and Search Console every day and reports on them. It changes a campaign only through a proposal that a person signs in the product. In your account, that person is me.

From €600 a month. I tell you the exact figure for your account once I've looked at it.

Ask for your price

It fits if

  • You already spend on Google Ads and want the account run by the person who reads it.
  • You spend enough for the settings to matter. With €300 a month they barely do; with €5,000 or €10,000, every setting is money.
  • You can tell me, in the first weeks, which leads were good.

How it works

  1. You write to me

    Tell me what you spend on Google Ads and what you'd like it to do.

  2. I look at the account

    You give me access, I read it, and I tell you what I see and what it would cost.

  3. The first weeks

    I'm persistent: I ask about lead quality, not just volume. You hire someone to stop thinking about ads, and the first job is to make you think about them a little more.

  4. After that

    The software reads the account every day and I make the changes. Your part becomes telling me: more of this, less of that.

Questions about hiring a Google Ads freelancer

How much does a Google Ads freelancer cost?

I charge from €600 a month. The exact figure depends on your account, and you get it once I've looked at it.

Freelancer or agency?

I'm a freelancer working through my company, Climent Media, which also has an agency side for teams that already spend. With me you get one person: the one who reads the account is the one who runs it, with no account manager in between.

Do you use AI to run the account?

Yes. I run your account myself, together with a team of AI agents. Climent Ads Assistant, the software I built, reads the account every day. The agents propose each change with its data and its risk, and none reaches your account until I sign it off, one by one. What the platform adjusts on its own, such as automated bidding, does not go through that sign-off. What you hire is the service, not the software.

Can I connect my own AI to my Google Ads account?

Yes. I've compared the Google Ads MCP options, and what each one can change in your account.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Spanish and Dutch, from Utrecht, remotely.
