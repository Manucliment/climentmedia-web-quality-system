Climent Ads Assistant · in testing

The Google Ads audit that runs inside the software.

The audit reads one Google Ads account, splits the spend by line of business and runs ten blocks of analysis on it. Every number in the report is computed by the software, not written by a model.

Want to see it on one account? Email me

The Paid Media Capacity Check is being built as the way in. One email with the link when it opens. No calendar, no sales call.

What the audit reads, and what it hands back

  • Spend split into lines of business, so a brand campaign and a prospecting campaign are not judged against the same number.
  • Ten blocks of analysis, and the places where they interfere with each other.
  • Search terms, keywords with their Quality Score, landing pages, ad groups and ads.
  • Findings as data: the block, the status and the line of business each one belongs to.
  • The columns it could not read, named. A blank is never passed off as a clean result.

What you tell it first, because Google does not know

  1. 1What the account is for: conversions, or spending a budget on relevant traffic
  2. 2The monthly budget, because Google only stores a daily one per campaign
  3. 3Your brand terms, so search terms split into brand and generic
  4. 4The margin per line, if the client gave it to you

The margin is what turns ROAS into money. With a 40% margin, anything under 2.5x loses money. No ad platform holds that number, so the audit never guesses it.

Meta: read, not scored yet

Climent Ads Assistant reads Meta: the ads with their creative and results, the breakdowns, and the settings. The audit engine does not score Meta yet.

So a Meta problem is not missing from the report because the account is clean. It is missing because nothing has checked it yet.

Who it is for, and who it is not

The audit is built for paid media agencies that are hiring media buyers, where the owner still reviews accounts and decides what each one needs first.

The audit is not for freelancers looking for more clients, agencies without a delivery team, or anyone after another reporting dashboard.

The connections it reads through

  • Google Ads MCP: the account the audit scores.
  • Meta Ads MCP: read next to it, not scored yet.
  • Google Analytics MCP: what the site recorded, against what the platforms claim.
  • Search Console MCP: the organic side of the same question.
  • Google Tag Manager MCP: which tag fires each conversion the audit counts.

If you would rather do it yourself today: The Ad Account Leak Check walks you through reading your own account step by step, free, no email. The Weekly Ads Decision System is the routine it belongs to, and Climent Ads Assistant is the console that runs it every week.

Before you ask

Which ad platforms does it audit?

Google Ads. Climent Ads Assistant also reads Meta, but the audit engine does not score it yet.

Does it change anything in the account?

The audit reads and reports. A change it points to, a negative keyword for example, becomes a proposal that a person has to sign before anything is sent.

Can I run it on my account today?

Not from this page. It runs inside Climent Ads Assistant, which is in testing. The Paid Media Capacity Check is being built to put a first answer on your screen, for one account. Email me and you get the link when it opens.
