Marca Real

Weekly gardening in Madrid

A regular gardener comes on the same day every week. They mow the lawn, trim the hedges, pull the weeds and take away everything that is left. If they cannot come one day, they let you know in good time and someone who already knows your garden comes instead, because every garden has its own sheet with the plants, the watering and what was done last time. There is no lock-in: you can stop whenever you like, with a message. We work across the city and in the towns around it.

From 40 € a month, and the first visit in 14 days.
