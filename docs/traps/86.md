## §86 · Four browser checks failed two one-page sites, and the brief blamed the wrong things

The same two sites as [§85](85.md): a personal page and a page explaining where a retired brand
went, each **one content page** plus privacy and cookies, on purpose. After upload, the door's
browser step failed both homes. The question was the right one — false positive of the gate, or a
defect of the site? — and each answer had to be measured, because the brief itself carried two
wrong premises.

| Check | What it said | Whose | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ENLACES` | 0 internal body links, minimum 2 | **the gate** | Nowhere to link: each sitemap lists the home and two legal pages. And the type's own reason for the minimum — *"the offer and the conversion"* — was met: the offer lives on another domain and the conversion is a `mailto:`, and this count counts neither |
| `VARIEDAD` | 3 and 2 layout classes, minimum 4 for `nosotros` | **the gate** for the 4 · **the site** for the 2 | The floor was justified as *"its 4 required roles"*, and a role is not a layout class. The canonical closing (mould 11) classifies as `otro`, which is never counted, and the moulds the house gives the other roles only produce three classes. **The house's own template for the type (01 + 02 + 06 + 11) measured 3 and failed.** With 3, the retired-brand page still fails: its context section is plain prose where the template puts an alternating pair |
| `ANCHO-MIN` | figure cells of 236 and 196 px, floor 240 | **the gate** | The 240 floor was measured on **card** cells; a figure's label passes the 20-character filter that was meant to exclude logos. Every figure fitted on one line, labels ran at 22–31 characters per line, and the house's own data band caps its label at 22ch (196 px) and lives in 167 px cells on a phone |
| density, 390 px | 856 px with no stop, on an 844 px screen | **the site** | The brief said the pull quote was a `<blockquote>` missing from the stop list. **It was a styled `<p>`.** The list did lack `blockquote` — fixed — but measured, the run stays at 856 px with it. A subheading splitting the story takes it to 467 px |

And the brief's second premise: *"the warnings failure follows from the others."* It did not. The
personal page had **three independent warnings** — type sizes of 15, 16, 17 and 18 px, a pixel apart;
too few reading paragraphs to compute the line-length median; 4 of 5 blocks with no image — and the
other page two, plus one derived from the variety count. Those belong to the sites, and the type scale
is a plain defect.

**The fixes, each with the case that keeps it honest:**

- **`ENLACES` has a ceiling: you cannot be asked to link more pages than exist.** The minimum becomes
  `min(the type's minimum, the OTHER content pages in the sitemap)`, without legal pages (the same
  expression the page-type inference already used, now shared). The sitemap is read from the page's
  own origin, synchronously, so the gate still returns its JSON in one pass. Three cautions, all on
  the side of the full rule: no readable sitemap (a 404, a sitemap index, a local `file://` copy) means
  no ceiling; **if the page itself links — nav and footer included — to a content page the sitemap
  does not list, the sitemap is incomplete and is not trusted**; and only `<url><loc>` in the sitemap
  namespace counts, because `<image:loc>` is an image. A capped minimum is always written into the
  notes with the list that capped it.
- **`nosotros` needs 3 layout classes, not 4** — the roles that *can* count, the same arithmetic that
  gave `hub` 3 and `gracias` 2 (roles minus the closing). With 4, passing required adding a section
  the anatomy does not ask for, which the type's own template forbids in writing.
- **A figure cell is not a card cell.** Its largest text carries a digit, is at most 12 characters and
  at least twice the size of the smallest text in the cell, and the cell has **no heading and no
  paragraph**. Measured at 1422 px over the 20 moulds, the grid fixtures and both sites: figure cells
  run ×2.27 to ×4; the pricing mould has a ×3.43 figure but also a heading and a paragraph, so it stays
  a card; every other card is ×1.14 to ×1.75. A figure cell is judged by what the data-band mould names
  as breaking it — **the figure must fit on one line and inside its cell** — and still counts as a grid
  for the container check, so a band squeezed by a CSS collision still fails.
- **`blockquote` is a stop.** By the principle already written in the density gate — anything that gives
  the eye somewhere to land — a quotation is a set-off block, the same family as `figure`, which was on
  the list. The stop is its **top edge**, so a quotation wrapping a wall of paragraphs keeps its run.

**Seen red first.** The new structure cases run against the previous gate give **18 OK · 5 MAL**:
the one-page site with its sitemap (EN1), the ceiling with one sibling page (EN2 — the old gate asked
for 2), the house's own `nosotros` template (NV1), the narrow figure band (K1) and the broken band's
new message (K2). Against the new gate, **23 OK · 0 MAL**. The guards are green on both and must stay
so: no sitemap (EN3), a sitemap that omits a page the nav links (EN4), the template with its context
in prose (NV2), cards with a big figure at the same width (K3). In the density bank the quotation in
mid-section (f10) fails the old gate and passes the new one, and its two guards — the same quotation
in a styled `<p>` (f11), a quotation wrapping a wall (f12) — fail on both: **32 OK · 0 MAL**, from 29.

**A red for the wrong reason, caught on the way.** The first version of K1 looked for `ANCHO-MIN`
anywhere in the output and went red with the gate right: the gate's *notes* also say `ANCHO-MIN` — the
container it could not check, the figure cells it judged by their figure. A failure line carries the
dot (`ANCHO-MIN ·`), and that is what the case anchors to now.

**And one defect of the battery itself, found by measuring the baseline from a clean copy.** The
structure battery composes its positive control into `work/`, which is not versioned; on a fresh clone
the folder does not exist, the composer could not write, **exited 0 anyway** because its last line was
an `echo`, and the next line **truncated the versioned `mould-home-fixed.html` to zero bytes**. P1 went
red and a tracked file was destroyed on every new clone. The battery now creates the folder, and the
composer fails when it wrote nothing.

**Where else the same rule applies** (box 3's question, measured): the minimal page each of the 13
types' anatomies describes, built from the moulds `roles.tsv` gives each role. **9 of 13 fail
`VARIEDAD`**, because the class is coarse on purpose — the feature grid, the steps, the data band, the
checklist, the social proof, the pricing tiers, the index, the flow chain and the gallery all measure as
`rejilla`. `nosotros` was the only type whose floor exceeded **the number of roles that can count**.
But `home` and `hub` hit the same wall one level down: their roles can count, yet the moulds those
roles are given produce at most three and two distinct classes (`home`: hero, `rejilla`,
`par-alterno`), so they reach their floors of 4 and 3 only with a section beyond the anatomy — which
is what the battery's home control carries, with nine moulds. **Not changed here, and declared**:
unlike `nosotros`, added the day before and used on no other site, those floors have been deciding
verdicts on live sites for weeks, and moving them needs the park measured first. Whether a page built
only from grid-family moulds is monotonous is a question about the class, not about one type's floor.

**Caught by:** `structure-gate-tests/battery.sh` (FRONTERA 7: EN1–EN4 · FRONTERA 8: NV1–NV2 ·
FRONTERA 9: K1–K3) and `measure-screens-tests/tests.sh` (f10, f11, f12).

**The cheap signal:** a FAIL that no design of the page could clear without inventing something — a
page, a section the anatomy does not ask for, a wider cell than its content needs. Before obeying a
threshold, ask what the site would have to become to pass it. And before trusting a brief's diagnosis,
open the element it names: here, the "blockquote" was a paragraph.
