## §89 · The browser gates ran after the upload, and the signed reason said the browser would answer

A new barbershop site went up with **10 FAILs out of 16–18 browser measurements**: the booking button
under the fold at 360, the desktop menu stacked into three and four rows on a phone, and a home page
whose hero only linked to the price list. Every one of those was measured, and none of them stopped
anything.

**Why.** `deploy.sh` ran the browser gates in step 6, **after** the line that uploads, and step 6 did
not block. Its own comment said why: "it has already been uploaded, and stopping here undoes
nothing". That is true, and it is also why a red there never stopped a deploy. The verdict went to
the history file and nowhere else.

**And the signature contradicted the measurement.** The same deploy carried an `--aun-asi` for an
unmeasured check whose reason was, in substance, "G11 and the browser answer this". The browser had
already answered, with FAIL. The door accepted any non-empty reason, so a reason that leaned on an
instrument saying the opposite was as good as a true one.

**Two smaller holes in the same place.**

- The final QA checklist said both "FAIL does not close" and "all three run against the
  **published** site". You cannot follow both before uploading.
- `mobile-gate.js` M1 counted any visible button, including one that only links to another page.
  It also only measured at 390 px, and the barbershop's booking button fell just under the fold
  at 360.

**The fix.**

- **Step 1-quater** runs the same gates on **the candidate**, before the upload line. The tree
  is copied to the measuring host and served from disk by `run-gate.js` under the production
  origin, so links, the sitemap and "same site" do not change.
  - The gates are structure, screens, mobile at 360/390/1366/1440 and the new
    `mobile-menu-gate.js`.
  - `"candidato": N` on the diagnostic line proves the candidate was served. With 0, the run
    measured production and counts as NOT MEASURED.
- A FAIL stops `--subir`. Only an `--aun-asi` that **names every red gate** lets it through, and
  `nav_decide` **rejects a reason that says the browser answers** when the browser said FAIL or
  measured nothing.
- On a site that declares `URLS_NAVEGADOR`, an unmeasured candidate also stops the upload,
  unless the reason says "navegador"/"browser".
- M1 now asks for an action that **converts**: a form, a phone number, a message app, a booking
  platform (`HOSTS_RESERVA`), or a contact/booking path on the same site. It also asks at four
  sizes.
- Step 6 stays, for what only the real host can answer: CDN, cache, headers, and the form
  against its backend.

**Before switching the block on, the published sites were swept with the new gates.** The sweep
found two false reds of the gates themselves, and both were fixed with a fixture seen red first:

- N1 counted the logo and the language links as menu rows.
- A sign-up on the site's own `app.` subdomain did not count as converting.

The sweep also found a transient `504` from a shared host that read as NOT MEASURED, so a
measurement without a verdict is now repeated once. What was left is recorded in the decision that
switched the block on.

**The cheap signal.** A gate whose verdict lands only in a log is a report, not a gate. Ask of every
check in the deploy path: *does a red here stop the line that uploads?* If the answer is "it is
after the line", it guards nothing.

**Caught by:**

- `deploy-tests/tests.sh`, block `nav_decide`, which runs the door's own bytes between its marks:
  - FAIL without `--aun-asi` does not upload;
  - a reason naming one of two red gates does not upload;
  - "the browser answers it" with a FAIL does not upload, in Spanish and in English;
  - naming the browser without leaning on it does;
  - naming `mobile-menu-gate` does not sign `mobile-gate`;
  - unmeasured with and without `URLS_NAVEGADOR`.
- `mobile-gate-tests/battery.sh`: the CAND-VERDE and CAND-ROJO cases, served from disk, and M1 at
  four sizes with conversion.
- `mobile-menu-gate-tests/battery.sh`.
