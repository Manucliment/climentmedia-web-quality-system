## §87 · An upload from an older base put another session's pages back, and the step that saw it did not stop

A site that several sessions deploy on the same day. At 11:55 one session uploaded two new guides
and the pages that link them: 139 files. At 12:04 another uploaded a regenerated page from a branch
cut **before** the first session merged. The door uploads the whole tree, so the second upload put
four of the first session's pages, its sitemap and its `llms.txt` back to their previous version.
The two new guide files stayed served, because an upload adds and overwrites and never deletes, but
nothing linked to them and the sitemap no longer listed them. It was repaired ten minutes later by
uploading the union: the new main plus the page, which file by file differed from the 11:55 tree
only in that page.

**Why it was silent.** Three instruments were running, and each told the truth about its own
question:

| Instrument | What it said | Why it did not help |
|---|---|---|
| Step 2-ter (`same-text.pl` against the served pages) | «5 con perdida», where the change touched one page | It printed the losses and uploaded: «NO se para la subida -- todavia --» |
| G11 | 135 of 135 identical | It compares what is served with the receipt, that is with what was just uploaded, not with what was there before |
| The operator's check before deploying | the base's tree hash matched the last served tree | It was taken 40 minutes earlier, and another upload happened in between |

The step had run 205 times since 26-aug: **58 with a loss (28%)**, 146 without. That is why the fix
is not a wall: removing text on purpose happens in a quarter of all uploads.

**The fix.** A loss now **stops the upload before the line that uploads** (exit 1: nothing served
changes and nothing is written to the history). The door names the pages and how many words each
loses, and says the two things a loss can mean:

- **Text you removed on purpose.** Declare it with `--loses-text "the reason"`, which writes the
  pages, the counts and the reason into the history, the same way `--anyway` records what was not
  measured.
- **A page your change does not touch.** That usually means another session uploaded after your
  base, and this upload would put its pages back. It is not declared: fetch, redo the change on top
  of what is new, and measure again.

The dry run still downloads nothing and does not stop. The stop happens before the upload line, so
the real run is its own preview.

**The cheap signal:** a loss on a page your change does not touch. And the check that prevents it
has to run right before uploading, not 40 minutes before: `git fetch`, and the last `SERVIDO` line of
the history (`receipt.pl --historial --sitio <url>`) against your base's tree hash
(`receipt.pl --arbol --repo <the base tree>`).

**Caught by:** `receipt-tests/tests-door.sh`, section 13 (an undeclared loss exits 1 and leaves the
served page and the history untouched; a declared one uploads and records the page, the count and
the reason; an upload with no loss is unchanged; the dry run does not stop; `--loses-text` is
accepted).
